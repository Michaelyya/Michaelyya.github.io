Hello World, I'm Michael Yongan Yu 👋

M.Sc. Computer Science · McGill University & Mila · AI4SG

I’m a first-year thesis-based master’s student in Computer Science at McGill University and , fortunate to be supervised by Prof. Jackie CK Cheung.

During my undergraduate studies, I was privileged to be supervised by Prof. Renee Sieber and Prof. Nikki G. Lobczowski. I also collaborated with Jingwei Ni at ETH Zurich and Prof. Markus Leippold at the University of Zurich.

My research focuses on evaluation frameworks for domain-adapted language models: how to involve experts in the loop and how to rigorously evaluate model adaptation. More broadly, I am interested in natural language processing, human–AI interaction, and responsible AI for real-world domains such as climate resilience and education.
