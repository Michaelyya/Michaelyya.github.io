I'm a first-year thesis-based master's student in [Computer Science](https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/) at <a href="https://www.mcgill.ca/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="logo-link"><span class="logo-lede">{% include inline_logo.liquid file='logo-mcgill.png' %}McGill</span> University</a> and <a href="https://mila.quebec/en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="logo-link">{% include inline_logo.liquid file='logo-mila.png' modifier='wordmark' alt='Mila' %}</a>, fortunate to be supervised by Prof. [Jackie CK Cheung](https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/~jcheung/index.html).

During my undergraduate studies, I was privileged to be supervised by Prof. [Renee Sieber](https://www.mcgill.ca/geography/people-0/sieber) and Prof. [Nikki G. Lobczowski](https://www.nikkilobczowski.com/home). I also collaborated with [Jingwei Ni](https://edisonni-hku.github.io/) at ETH Zurich and Prof. [Markus Leippold](https://www.df.uzh.ch/en/people/professor/leippold.html) at <a href="https://www.uzh.ch/en.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="logo-link"><span class="logo-lede">{% include inline_logo.liquid file='logo-uzh.png' modifier='adapt' %}the</span> University of Zurich</a>.

My research focuses on **evaluation frameworks for domain-adapted language models**: how to involve experts in the loop and how to rigorously evaluate model adaptation. More broadly, I am interested in natural language processing, human–AI interaction, and responsible AI for real-world domains such as climate resilience and education.
