Hi! I'm a first-year thesis-based master's student in [**Computer Science**](https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/) at **McGill University**. I am fortunate to be supervised by Prof. [**Jackie CK Cheung**](https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/~jcheung/index.html).

My research focuses on **valuation frameworks for domain-adapted language models**: how to involve experts in the loop and how to rigorously evaluate model adaptation. More broadly, I am interested in natural language processing, human–AI interaction, and responsible AI for real-world domains such as climate resilience and education.
